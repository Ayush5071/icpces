About the Conference

The Department of Electrical Engineering, Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology Allahabad has successfully organized eight successive students' conferences titled: "Students' Conference on Engineering and Systems (ICPCES 2012, ICPCES-2013, ICPCES-2014, ICPCES-2015, ICPCES-2019, ICPCES 2020, ICPCES-2022 & ICPCES-2024)", which were technically sponsored by the IEEE Uttar Pradesh Section and co-sponsored by the IEEE Joint Society Chapter of IE/PEL/CS (Industrial Electronics/ Power Electronics/ Control System). Department is organizing the ninth sequel conference of ICPCES to motivate student scholars working in the area of Engineering and Systems. The conference will focus on the emerging areas in the field of Power, Energy & Control, Electronics, Communication & Signal Processing, Computational Intelligence & Machine Learning, Mechanical Systems and Mechatronics, Applied Sciences, etc. The aim of the conference is to provide a platform for research students to present original work done by them which leads to sharing of knowledge at a highly technical platform. The student researchers and academicians from various reputed institutes will be invited for presentations, deliberations, and discussions. It is proposed to have a 3-day program during May 15th â€“ 17th, 2026. The technical program will comprise of keynote and tutorial sessions delivered by academic and industry experts of repute, and involve students' research work through oral presentations and technical exhibitions, etc.

About Electrical Engineering Department

The Electrical Engineering Department (EED) came into existence in the year 1961, with the objective to produce technical man power of high quality and promote research and development activity. With a modest beginning of introducing four year BE degree course in 1961, a post graduate programme in Electrical Machine / Power System / Control System was introduced in the year 1970-71. Currently, EED offers courses leading to a Bachelor of Technology in Electrical Engineering, Post Graduate and Ph.D programs in (i) Power Electronics and Drives (ii) Control & Instrumentation and (iii) Power System. The department has qualified and experienced faculty in all the related fields of Electrical Engineering viz. Electrical Power System, Electrical Machines, Control & Instrumentation and Power Electronics. The vision of the Department is to produce globally competitive technical manpower with sound knowledge of theory and practice, with a commitment to serve the society and to foster cutting edge research in Electrical Engineering pertaining to the problems currently faced by the country and the world.

AI-Driven Intelligent Systems for Smart Power, Embedded and Communication Technologies

The rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Internet of Things (IoT), Embedded Systems, and Smart Communication technologies has significantly transformed the domains of power systems, automation, industrial control, and intelligent computing. Modern industries and smart infrastructures increasingly rely on intelligent embedded platforms for efficient monitoring, predictive maintenance, real-time decision-making, energy optimization, and autonomous control. The convergence of AI with power electronics, control engineering, and embedded systems has opened new research opportunities in smart grids, sustainable energy management, cyber-physical systems, intelligent transportation, healthcare automation, and industrial IoT.

This special session aims to provide an interdisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and practitioners to present innovative research findings, emerging trends, and practical applications in intelligent embedded and communication technologies. The session will particularly encourage contributions focusing on AI-enabled smart systems, edge computing, data-driven optimization techniques, secure communication architectures, and intelligent automation for next-generation engineering applications.

Key Objectives

  • To promote advanced research in AI-driven smart power and embedded technologies.
  • To encourage interdisciplinary collaboration among researchers from electrical, electronics, computer science, and communication engineering domains.
  • To discuss recent advancements in intelligent control systems, IoT-enabled automation, and data-centric embedded solutions.
  • To explore innovative approaches for sustainable, efficient, and secure smart systems.
  • To provide a global platform for presenting high-quality research contributions aligned with Industry 4.0 and smart infrastructure development.
